The problem

Bangladesh floods annually, and response depends on knowing — quickly and accurately — where the water is and what it has damaged. Satellite imagery holds that answer if it can be turned into reliable maps.

What was done

  • Flood mapping using remote sensing and machine learning: building flood maps from satellite imagery
  • Damage estimation by image analysis, with machine-learning models applied to the imagery
  • Additional research work on Generative AI and its societal impact
