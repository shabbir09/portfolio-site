The problem

Real Fibre Glass Industries — an FRP manufacturer founded in 1994 — has a web shop that looks close to launch but leaks trust and sales underneath: product data errors, placeholder images and contacts, and a cart that cannot complete the basics. Its inventory still runs without a leak-proof digital system.

What was built (so far)

  • A full, two-pass audit of the live site (15 Nov 2026): homepage, 89-product catalogue, shop, cart, checkout, company and journal pages, map and contacts
  • A prioritised fix list separating the launch blocker from data-correctness and credibility work
  • A plan of action for the engagement: website + Facebook page management, then gradual inventory digitisation (assess → analyse → fix → install), with a focused web-only product line from the most suitable products as a later phase

Key audit findings

  • Launch blocker: cart quantity (+/−) and Remove controls do not respond — a customer cannot change quantity or remove an item.
  • Catalogue split: 89 products — 39 priced "Ready Stock", 50 "Request Quote"; prices carry no unit (per sft vs per piece).
  • Product descriptions are systematically shifted by one product across a whole product family; most product images are "NO IMAGE" placeholders.
  • No search; price sorting breaks on quote items; placeholder emails/phones still live; company-page claims unsubstantiated.
