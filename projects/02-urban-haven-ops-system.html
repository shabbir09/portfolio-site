The problem

A small short-stay operation (one flat in Bashundhara, Dhaka — two rooms listed on Airbnb, direct bookings on top) was being run from memory and chat threads: no single view of bookings or revenue, and every enquiry handled from scratch.

What was built

  • A live bookings-and-revenue dashboard for the property, with automatic daily Airbnb iCal sync for both listed rooms (ER1 + ER2)
  • A Facebook Page + WhatsApp Business front desk chosen deliberately over a heavy PMS at this volume: Page as the shop window, WhatsApp catalogue / quick replies / labels as the booking pipeline
  • A content and promo system on the same rails: Page cleanup executed, rate-card post pinned, approved promo posts distributed to Airbnb and Bashundhara community groups

How it works

  1. Airbnb bookings sync into the dashboard automatically each morning; direct (WhatsApp/Facebook) bookings are entered once and live in the same view.
  2. Enquiries arrive on the Page or WhatsApp and are handled with the quick-reply pack and label pipeline.
  3. Revenue is tracked per room per month against costs — margin, not vanity bookings, is the metric.

Proof / outcomes

  • Dashboard live; iCal connected for both rooms with a clean first sync on 20 Nov 2026.
  • November 2026 earnings of ৳31,300 recorded on the dashboard as of 20 Nov 2026.
  • Honest limit: the iCal feed carries no payout amounts — Airbnb payout figures are entered manually when they land. The business target (৳100–110k/month) is a target, not a result, and is labelled as such everywhere.
