HVAC Revenue System — Missed-Call Rescue + Database Reactivation on GoHighLevel

A reusable GoHighLevel system that answers missed calls with AI and reactivates a company's dormant customer list, built once as a template and deployable per client from a verified snapshot.

The problem

HVAC and home-service companies lose booked jobs in two quiet places: calls that ring out when the team is on a job, and past customers sitting untouched in the CRM for months. Both are follow-up problems, not advertising problems — the leads already exist.

What was built

A complete GoHighLevel template ("Orion Template — HVAC") covering both leaks, packaged as a snapshot so a new client deployment starts from a proven base instead of a blank account.

  • 12 workflows: missed-call rescue, speed-to-lead, booking, no-show recovery, estimate follow-up, review requests, database reactivation
  • Conversation AI agent "Front Desk" with a 9-FAQ knowledge base, running in Suggestive mode (human approves replies before autopilot)
  • Voice AI agent "Front Desk — Voice" (voice: Jessica, GPT-4.1) that books into the Service Booking calendar
  • HVAC sales pipeline (11 stages), custom fields, tag taxonomy, DBR Smart Lists
  • Two productised offers on top of the same system: Missed-Call Rescue and Database Reactivation (live as Fiverr gigs and Upwork projects since 18 Nov 2026)

How it works

  1. A call is missed → the system texts back within moments and the AI front desk continues the conversation toward a booking.
  2. A web form or chat lead arrives → speed-to-lead workflow engages before a competitor does.
  3. Dormant contacts are segmented into Smart Lists → a reactivation campaign runs with opt-out discipline (do-not-contact removes a contact from all workflows).
  4. Bookings land on the Service Booking calendar; no-shows and estimates get their own follow-up flows; happy customers get a review request.

Proof / outcomes

  • Snapshot "Orion HVAC Revenue System v1.1" (id 0B6oURMfQD2pqLDfpjp1) was load-tested in a fresh sub-account on 17 Nov 2026: all 12 workflows, the pipeline, and both AI agents arrived intact — including the Voice AI configuration, which a v1.0 test caught not travelling and which v1.1 fixed and re-verified.
  • Honest limit: there is no paying client deployment yet. This is a built, tested system and two live storefront gigs — not a client case study, and it is never presented as one.
